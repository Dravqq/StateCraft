
StateCraft

StateCraft is a strategy game project focused on managing a country, making political decisions, and shaping the future of a nation.

The goal is to create a strategic experience where decisions matter and players can develop their own approach to governing a state.

About the Project

StateCraft is a game development project focused on strategy, state management, and decision-making. The project is being actively developed, with the goal of expanding its mechanics and creating a deeper strategic experience.

Features

Planned features may include:

- State Management — Manage and develop your country.
- Strategic Decisions — Make decisions that influence the direction of your state.
- Politics and Governance — Shape your country's political future.
- Economic Development — Develop your state's economy and resources.
- Continuous Development — New mechanics and improvements as the project evolves.

Features are subject to change during development.
